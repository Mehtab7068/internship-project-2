CASE STUDY 01 Live

Google AI Interview

A mock interview platform for practicing with text, code and voice.

My role

I built a full-stack interview platform split between a React app, Express API and FastAPI AI service.

The challenge

Voice answers needed a reliable path from browser microphone capture to transcription and useful evaluation.

What I learned

I learned how audio processing, service boundaries and practical safeguards fit together in an AI feature.

The browser records WebM/Opus audio. The server uses FFmpeg to convert it to mono 16 kHz WAV. Gemini and AssemblyAI support scoring, confidence evaluation, structured feedback and dynamic questions, with retries and rate limiting. The app uses JWT and Google OAuth, Helmet, a 10 MB audio limit and restricted CORS. It is deployed on Vercel and Render.

CASE STUDY 02 Live

InterviewAI Strategy

A job-specific preparation plan based on a resume and role description.

My role

I built a full-stack platform that connects resume and job description analysis with interview preparation.

The challenge

The output needed to turn a long job description into a clear match view and concrete practice material.

What I learned

I learned to make AI output more useful by pairing it with a match visualizer, skill gaps and a roadmap.

Gemini compares a job description of up to 5,000 characters with an uploaded resume or profile. The platform shows a match score and skill-gap analysis, then generates stack-specific technical and behavioral questions. It also includes JWT authentication, saved dashboards, roadmap export and summary downloads.
